4. Draw the signature

Sign with your mouse, finger or stylus. The signature is automatically cropped and reduced to fit a compact area (maximum 90 × 32 PDF points). Click the center of the empty signature box; the tool cannot automatically detect box boundaries or nearby text.

The signed PDF includes an image-based handwritten electronic signature. It is not a certificate-backed digital signature.
